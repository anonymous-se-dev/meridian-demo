Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Meridian Financial Group ("Meridian", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies across our websites and online banking platforms, and how you can manage your preferences.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help sites function securely, remember your settings, understand usage, and deliver relevant content and advertising.

Managing your preferences

Your choices are captured and honoured by our Consent Management Platform, powered by Securiti. Update them anytime via , or "Your Privacy Choices" in the footer. Strictly necessary cookies — including those that protect your account and prevent fraud — cannot be disabled.

Consent management & Google Consent Mode

Meridian uses the Securiti Cookie Consent Management platform to capture and honour your choices, and implements Google Consent Mode v2 so tags loaded through Google Tag Manager respect your selection before setting non-essential cookies. Your consent record — the categories allowed and a timestamp — is retained as evidence of consent.
